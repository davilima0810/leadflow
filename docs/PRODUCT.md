# LeadFlow - Product Overview

## Product

LeadFlow is a B2B SaaS for lead qualification through conversational forms.

Companies create public links for potential customers. Visitors answer a configured sequence of questions in a chat-like interface. At the end, LeadFlow stores the lead, stores each answer, generates a structured summary, and may redirect the visitor to the company's WhatsApp with a pre-filled message.

## Core Value Proposition

Deliver qualified leads to sales teams, reducing time spent on repetitive discovery questions.

## Target Segments

The product must remain segment-agnostic. Initial examples include:

- car rental companies;
- real estate companies;
- clinics;
- repair shops;
- solar energy companies;
- restaurants;
- service providers;
- marketing agencies.

## Main Actors

- Company: tenant that owns users, flows, questions, and leads.
- User: authenticated company user who manages flows and reads leads.
- Visitor: unauthenticated person who answers a public flow.
- Lead: qualified opportunity created from a visitor conversation.

## Product Principles

- Optimize for the first paying customer.
- Keep the MVP simple and operational.
- Prefer generic flows over segment-specific hardcoding.
- Avoid complex automation until there is concrete demand.
- Use WhatsApp links initially, not the official WhatsApp Business API.

## Out Of Scope Initially

- AI qualification;
- CRM suite;
- billing and subscriptions;
- official WhatsApp Business API integration;
- mobile app;
- advanced analytics;
- drag-and-drop builder;
- agency management;
- complex automations.
